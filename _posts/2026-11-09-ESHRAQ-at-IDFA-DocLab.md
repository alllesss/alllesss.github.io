ESHRAQ is at [IDFA DocLab](https://festival.idfa.nl/en/film/88ea9b87-760b-488b-a921-99bcbc8a493e/ESHRAQ/) in Amsterdam, from 13 to 22 November, as part of the free DocLab Exhibition at De Brakke Grond.

It is a simulation inspired by Suhrawardi's 12th-century philosophy of Illumination. You don't play a character. You enter as a Soul, take possession of different bodies, and move through worlds shaped by spells of light, among them an Amsterdam canal and the Ziggurat of Ur. Less a game you finish than a place you return to, a cognitive playground.

## Two ways in

**Play** ESHRAQ yourself, or **watch** the artist walkthrough: two hours, looped, in which I move through its worlds and talk about how they came to be.

## Visiting

- **Where:** De Brakke Grond, Nes 45, Amsterdam
- **When:** Fri 13 to Sat 21 November, 11:30–20:30 · Sun 22 November, 11:30–18:00
- **Entry:** Free, with an online reservation for the day you visit
- **Reserve:** [ESHRAQ on the IDFA site](https://festival.idfa.nl/en/film/88ea9b87-760b-488b-a921-99bcbc8a493e/ESHRAQ/) (pick your day) or [idfa.nl/expo](https://idfa.nl/expo)
- **Also:** Guided tour · DocLab Playrooms on Mon 16 November, 15:00–17:00

ESHRAQ is produced by K.O. Productions, with sound and music by Shahin Entezami, and supported by Creative Industries Fund NL, the Ministry of Education, Culture and Science, and CIIIC.
