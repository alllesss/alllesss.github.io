ESHRAQ is at [IDFA DocLab](https://festival.idfa.nl/en/film/88ea9b87-760b-488b-a921-99bcbc8a493e/ESHRAQ/) in Amsterdam, from 13 to 22 November, as part of the free DocLab Exhibition at De Brakke Grond.


## Trailers

**Story trailer**

Story mode is the guided way into ESHRAQ. Over three chapters you wake as a Soul, learn to take a body, and travel from a quiet Carevansarai to the Ziggurat of Ur and on to an Amsterdam canal. Two voices go with you: SYS, the system that hosts the simulation, and the Soul itself, speaking in my voice. When the story ends, the rest of ESHRAQ is yours to explore.

**Gameplay trailer**

If you would rather watch than play, there is the walkthrough: a two-hour video I recorded and edited myself, playing on a loop at the exhibition. In it I move through ESHRAQ's levels one by one and talk about how they came to be: the ideas behind them, the references, and a few personal rants.

## Visiting

- **Where:** De Brakke Grond, Nes 45, Amsterdam
- **When:** Fri 13 to Sat 21 November, 11:30–20:30 · Sun 22 November, 11:30–18:00
- **Entry:** Free, with an online reservation for the day you visit
- **Reserve:** [ESHRAQ on the IDFA site](https://festival.idfa.nl/en/film/88ea9b87-760b-488b-a921-99bcbc8a493e/ESHRAQ/) (pick your day) or [idfa.nl/expo](https://idfa.nl/expo)
- **Also:** Guided tour · DocLab Playrooms on Mon 16 November, 17:45–18:45 at De Brakke Grond

ESHRAQ is produced by K.O. Productions, with sound and music by Shahin Entezami, and supported by Creative Industries Fund NL, the Ministry of Education, Culture and Science, and CIIIC.
